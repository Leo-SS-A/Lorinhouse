Our Story

From humble beginnings to global recognition, our journey is rooted in a passion for marble craftsmanship.

Our Founding

LorinHouse was founded in 2010 by master craftsman Lorenzo Bianchi, whose family has been working with marble for three generations. What began as a small workshop in Tuscany has grown into an internationally recognized brand, known for our commitment to quality and timeless design.

Lorenzo's vision was simple: to create marble furniture that honors the natural beauty of the stone while incorporating modern design sensibilities. He believed that marble furniture should not just be functional, but a work of art that tells a story and lasts for generations.

Today, we remain true to that vision, combining traditional Italian craftsmanship with contemporary design to create pieces that elevate any space.

Our Craftsmanship

At LorinHouse, every piece is crafted by hand by our team of skilled artisans, each with at least 10 years of experience working with marble. We source only the finest natural marble from quarries in Italy, Greece, and Spain, ensuring that each slab meets our rigorous quality standards.

Our process begins with selecting the perfect marble slab, taking into account the veining, color, and texture to ensure it's suited for its intended use. The slab is then cut, shaped, and polished using a combination of traditional hand tools and modern machinery to achieve the perfect finish.

Each piece undergoes multiple quality checks throughout the production process, and only when it meets our exacting standards is it deemed ready for our customers.

Quality
